# Brutalist Skill (Neo-Brutalist & High-Energy)

Guides the agent to build punchy, eye-catching, high-contrast layouts reminiscent of neo-brutalism and modern bold marketing sites.

---

## 1. Visual Signatures

* **Hard Shadows**: Solid offset drop shadows with zero blur:
  ```css
  box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #000000;
  /* Hover state: slight press */
  transform: translate(2px, 2px);
  box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px #000000;
  ```
* **Borders**: Crisp, uniform solid black or dark borders (`2px solid #0f172a` or `3px solid #000000`).
* **High Contrast Colors**: Vivid pops of yellow (`#facc15`), acid green (`#4ade80`), electric blue, or punchy coral against high-contrast black and white.

---

## 2. Typography & Badges

* **Heavy Weights**: `font-weight: 800` or `900` for titles and callouts.
* **Punchy Stickers / Pills**: Rounded or rectangular sticker badges with thick borders and solid shadows for promo announcements, discounts, and guarantees.
* **Uppercase Accents**: Judicious use of uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.05em`) for categories, step counters, and urgency tags.
