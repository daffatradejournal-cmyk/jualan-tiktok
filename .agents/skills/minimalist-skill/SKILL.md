# Minimalist Skill (Editorial & Restrained Design)

Directs the agent to design high-clarity interfaces with zero ornamental clutter, prioritizing typography, whitespace, and sharp functional focus.

---

## 1. Principles of Restraint

* **Eliminate Visual Noise**: Remove unnecessary cards, excessive divider lines, and decorative gradients. Let whitespace separate sections.
* **Typographic Hierarchy**: Let scale and font weight do the work instead of colored background boxes.
* **Monochrome Foundation**: Build 90% of the interface in pure monochrome (stark black, deep charcoal, off-white, subtle slate lines) before applying any single accent color.

---

## 2. Spacing System

* Generous section padding (`80px` to `120px` desktop, `48px` to `64px` mobile).
* Tight component groupings: label, value, and action close together (`gap: 8px` to `12px`).
* Wide line heights for body copy (`line-height: 1.6` to `1.75`) with constrained max-width (`65ch` max for prose).

---

## 3. High-Conversion Simplicity

* **Direct CTAs**: Single unmistakable button or WhatsApp link per view.
* **Transparent Pricing**: Large clear pricing figures with breakdown of what is included and excluded.
* **Immediate Proof**: Real customer quotes, badges, or guarantee badges without carousel gimmicks.
