# Workspace Guidelines & Design Directives

This project (`jualan kita`) is a high-conversion Indonesian digital product & setup services landing page (NPWP, WPS Office PC Level Android Tablet, etc.).

## 🎨 Taste & Frontend Design Standards

All UI, frontend, and landing page work in this project must adhere to **Taste Skill** guidelines:

### 1. Core Principles
- **No Generic AI Slop**: Avoid clichéd 3-column identical card grids, default purple/indigo linear gradients, centered headline stacks on every section, and placeholder copy.
- **Audience & Context**: Target Indonesian digital consumers. Use natural, high-converting Indonesian phrasing (*"Garansi Resmi"*, *"Langsung Pakai"*, *"Bisa COD / Pembayaran Fleksibel"*, *"Konsultasi WhatsApp Cepat"*).
- **Mobile First**: Over 85% of traffic is on mobile. Ensure sticky WhatsApp CTAs are thumb-accessible, tap targets are minimum 44px, and modal sheets are smooth.

### 2. Available Workspace Skills
The following skills are installed and available on-demand in `.agents/skills/`:
- `design-taste-frontend`: Core anti-slop frontend art direction & brief inference.
- `soft-skill`: High-end Apple/Linear-style tactile interfaces with haptic depth and spring micro-interactions.
- `minimalist-skill`: High-contrast, clean typographic hierarchy with disciplined whitespace.
- `brutalist-skill`: Punchy neo-brutalist styling with bold borders, hard offset shadows, and vivid accents.
- `redesign-skill`: Audit-first UX/UI redesign framework.
- `output-skill`: Production-grade full-output delivery without lazy stubs.
